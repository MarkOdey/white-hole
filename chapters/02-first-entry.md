# First entry — age 10

*Draft 1. Level 2 (her diary), Christmas, age 10.*

---

Dear diary,

It is christmas today. I received you as a gift from my mother, and I think I know why.

A while ago I asked her about my grandfather. She admitted she doesn't know much about him — that she spent little time with him. So I asked the others too, and it turned out no one in our family really knew about him either. What he liked. What he was like. Anything.

I got a little frightened realising that my grandfather was long forgotten. He was a whole person once. Now he is just a name we almost never say.

I couldn't stop thinking about it, so tonight I asked her.

— Mom, I don't want to be forgotten. Promise me I will be remembered after I die.

— I promise my love.

She didn't laugh, or tell me not to be morbid on christmas. She just promised, the way you promise something you mean. I love my mother. She always knows the right thing to do at the right time for me.

Everyone else is downstairs still. Dad has the TV too loud and my brother is crying about something. Nobody has noticed I came up. That is alright. It means it is just you and me up here.

So here is my plan, diary. We are going to write down everything. Every day, if I can manage it. Then it won't matter if I get forgotten, because there will be proof I was here, in my own hand.

And one day I am going to do something so big that nobody could forget me even if they tried. Something far away. I haven't worked out what yet.

But we have time.

Merry christmas.
