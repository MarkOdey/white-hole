# Opening — first page

*Draft 1. Level 1 (the entity), direct address.*

---

Oh. Oh, look at you. Look at you!

Come, sit — sit down, there, make yourself at house.

Heh. No, no, I am only pleased. It has been a while, that is all. Rather a while. I am out of practice at the greeting part of things; you will have to be patient with an old — well. With me.

A human. That is the word, yes? Human. Heh — I only ever had the one to ask about it, and she was not always so certain of it herself.

It was loud once, you know. Oh, it was very loud. Everything happening and all of it happening at the same time, and me in the middle of it like a fellow with too few hands, and I remember thinking — I remember thinking quite clearly — that what I should like is a bit of quiet.

Heh.

Well. There you are. I got it.

...I knew one of you. Did I say? I knew one of you, once. Not long. Not long for me — I am a poor judge of it, mind. Long enough for her.

Oh, she had a peculiar mind, that one. Bright. Very, very bright. And peculiar, which is not the same thing at all, and is much the rarer of the two. She noticed all the wrong things — ha! — by which I mean the things nobody else could be troubled to notice. By which I mean, as it turned out, the right ones.

I was very fond of her.

She is long passed now.

Ah.

Well.

You have a bit of time, haven't you. You have the look of someone with a bit of time.
