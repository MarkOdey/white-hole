# Opening — first page

*Draft 1. Level 1 (the entity), direct address.*

---

Oh. Oh, look at you. Look at you!

Come, sit — sit down, there, make yourself confortable.

Sorry if I may be intimidating you. No, no, I am only pleased. It has been a while, that is all. Rather a while. I am out of practice at the greeting part of things; you will have to be patient with an old creature like me.

A human. That is the word, yes? Human. Eh! I remember very well only one before I had the chance to share a moment with.

It was loud once, you know. They were times of great changes. Oh, it was very loud. Everything happening and all of it happening at the same time, and me in the middle like maestro wiggling my wand. 

Time is much more peaceful these days.
Heh.

Well. There you are. Look at you filled with curious thoughts. I have so much to say yet maybe none of it matters. 

I can sense that maybe you would like to know more about that other human? You humans always thinking that you are in the center of the world! eh eh. Well you are not wrong to be so proud. It is a rarety to witness carbon life in such an intricate configuration.


Oh, she had a peculiar mind, that one. Bright. Very, very bright. And peculiar, which is not the same thing at all, and is much the rarer of the two. She noticed all the wrong things — ha! — by which I mean the things nobody else could be troubled to notice. By which I mean, as it turned out, the right ones.

I was very fond of her.

She is long passed now.

Ah.

Well.

You have a bit of time, haven't you. You have the look of someone with a bit of time.
